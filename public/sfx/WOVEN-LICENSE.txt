Klipe bundles 35 unmodified WAV files, renamed and categorized for the library.
woven-library.json lists the original source URL, duration and SHA-256 for each
file. Only the sound assets are included; no EDIT-IA-PRO program code, proprietary
music collection or paid ElevenLabs generation is included.

The collection was obtained from the user's supplied EDIT-IA-PRO archive.
File hashes were checked against its Woven manifest. The upstream license was
independently checked on 2026-10-11. Existing personal audio is not replaced.
